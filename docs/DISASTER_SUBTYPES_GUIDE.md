# 🎯 Disaster Sub-Types Guide

## Overview

Each disaster type has multiple sub-types that significantly affect resource requirements. Selecting the correct sub-type improves prediction accuracy by 15-30%.

---

## 🌊 Flood Sub-Types

### 1. **Urban Flood**
- **Characteristics**: City areas, drainage overflow, localized
- **Resource Impact**: Lower shelter need (buildings available), moderate water need
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.0×, Shelter 0.3×, Medical 1.0×, Water 0.8×
- **Examples**: Mumbai floods (2005), Chennai floods (2015)

### 2. **Riverine Flood**
- **Characteristics**: River overflow, large area affected, gradual onset
- **Resource Impact**: High water need, widespread shelter requirement
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.2×, Shelter 1.0×, Medical 1.0×, Water 1.2×
- **Examples**: Kerala floods (2018), Bihar floods (annual)

### 3. **Flash Flood**
- **Characteristics**: Sudden, intense, rapid onset, high velocity
- **Resource Impact**: Critical rescue need, high shelter demand, immediate medical response
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.5×, Shelter 1.5×, Medical 1.3×, Water 1.0×
- **Examples**: Uttarakhand floods (2013), Srikakulam flash floods

### 4. **Coastal Flood**
- **Characteristics**: Storm surge, tidal influence, coastal areas
- **Resource Impact**: Moderate impact, evacuation common
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.0×, Shelter 0.7×, Medical 0.9×, Water 1.0×
- **Examples**: Cyclone-induced flooding in coastal Odisha, West Bengal

## 🏚️ Earthquake Sub-Types

### 1. **Shallow Earthquake** (<70km depth)
- **Characteristics**: Near surface, more surface damage, higher intensity felt
- **Resource Impact**: High shelter need, critical medical response, widespread damage
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.2×, Shelter 1.3×, Medical 1.4×, Water 1.1×
- **Examples**: Latur earthquake (1993), Kutch earthquake (2001)

### 2. **Deep Earthquake** (>70km depth)
- **Characteristics**: Deep focus, less surface impact, lower intensity
- **Resource Impact**: Reduced resource needs, localized impact
- **Modifiers**: Food 0.9×, Shelter 0.8×, Medical 0.9×, Water 0.9×
- **Examples**: Deep focus earthquakes in Northeast India

### 3. **Tectonic Earthquake**
- **Characteristics**: Plate boundary movement, widespread shaking
- **Resource Impact**: Large area affected, significant shelter and medical need
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.1×, Shelter 1.2×, Medical 1.2×, Water 1.0×
- **Examples**: Most major Indian earthquakes

### 4. **Volcanic Earthquake**
- **Characteristics**: Associated with volcanic activity, ash fall, gas hazards
- **Resource Impact**: Critical medical need (respiratory issues), high shelter demand
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.3×, Shelter 1.4×, Medical 1.5×, Water 1.3×
- **Examples**: Andaman & Nicobar volcanic activity

## 🌀 Cyclone Sub-Types

### 1. **Tropical Cyclone** (Category 1-3)
- **Characteristics**: Standard tropical cyclone, wind speeds 74-178 km/h
- **Resource Impact**: Moderate to high resource needs
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.1×, Shelter 1.2×, Medical 1.1×, Water 1.1×
- **Examples**: Most Bay of Bengal cyclones

### 2. **Extratropical Cyclone**
- **Characteristics**: Weaker system, cooler temperatures, less intense
- **Resource Impact**: Lower resource requirements
- **Modifiers**: Food 0.9×, Shelter 0.9×, Medical 0.9×, Water 0.9×
- **Examples**: Winter cyclones in North India

### 3. **Super Cyclone** (Category 4-5)
- **Characteristics**: Extreme intensity, wind speeds >178 km/h, catastrophic damage
- **Resource Impact**: Extreme resource needs, critical rescue operations
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.5×, Shelter 1.6×, Medical 1.4×, Water 1.3×
- **Examples**: Cyclone Fani (2019), Cyclone Amphan (2020), Cyclone Hudhud (2014)

### 4. **Tropical Depression**
- **Characteristics**: Weak system, wind speeds <63 km/h
- **Resource Impact**: Minimal resource needs
- **Modifiers**: Food 0.7×, Shelter 0.6×, Medical 0.7×, Water 0.8×
- **Examples**: Early-stage cyclonic systems

## ⛰️ Landslide Sub-Types

### 1. **Rockfall**
- **Characteristics**: Falling rocks, localized, high trauma risk
- **Resource Impact**: High medical need, low shelter need (localized)
- **Modifiers**: Food 0.8×, Shelter 0.7×, Medical 1.2×, Water 0.8×
- **Examples**: Himalayan rockfalls, road blockages

### 2. **Mudslide**
- **Characteristics**: Widespread mud flow, large area affected
- **Resource Impact**: High shelter need, significant food/water requirements
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.3×, Shelter 1.4×, Medical 1.1×, Water 1.2×
- **Examples**: Kerala landslides (2018), Himachal Pradesh mudslides

### 3. **Debris Flow**
- **Characteristics**: Fast-moving, destructive, high velocity
- **Resource Impact**: Critical rescue need, high shelter and medical demand
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.4×, Shelter 1.5×, Medical 1.3×, Water 1.1×
- **Examples**: Malin landslide (2014), Uttarakhand debris flows

### 4. **Rotational Landslide**
- **Characteristics**: Slower movement, predictable, curved failure surface
- **Resource Impact**: Moderate resource needs, allows preparation time
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.1×, Shelter 1.2×, Medical 1.0×, Water 1.0×
- **Examples**: Slow-moving landslides in hilly regions

## 🔥 Wildfire Sub-Types

### 1. **Forest Fire**
- **Characteristics**: Large forested areas, difficult access, long duration
- **Resource Impact**: High water need, significant shelter for evacuees
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.2×, Shelter 1.3×, Medical 1.1×, Water 1.4×
- **Examples**: Simlipal forest fires, Bandipur forest fires

### 2. **Grassland Fire**
- **Characteristics**: Smaller impact, faster spread, easier control
- **Resource Impact**: Lower resource needs, localized impact
- **Modifiers**: Food 0.9×, Shelter 0.8×, Medical 0.9×, Water 1.2×
- **Examples**: Grassland fires in Rajasthan, Gujarat

### 3. **Urban Fire**
- **Characteristics**: High population density, building fires, rapid spread
- **Resource Impact**: Critical shelter need, high medical response, water for firefighting
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.4×, Shelter 1.5×, Medical 1.3×, Water 1.3×
- **Examples**: Building fires in Mumbai, Delhi

### 4. **Wildland-Urban Interface Fire**
- **Characteristics**: Mixed urban and wildland, high risk, complex response
- **Resource Impact**: High shelter and medical need, significant water requirement
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.3×, Shelter 1.4×, Medical 1.2×, Water 1.3×
- **Examples**: Fires near hill stations, forest-adjacent urban areas

## ☀️ Drought Sub-Types

### 1. **Meteorological Drought**
- **Characteristics**: Low rainfall, prolonged dry period
- **Resource Impact**: High water need, moderate food requirement
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.0×, Shelter 0.5×, Medical 0.8×, Water 1.5×
- **Examples**: Annual droughts in Rajasthan, Gujarat

### 2. **Agricultural Drought**
- **Characteristics**: Crop failure, food shortage, economic impact
- **Resource Impact**: Critical food need, high water requirement, low shelter need
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.3×, Shelter 0.4×, Medical 0.7×, Water 1.6×
- **Examples**: Marathwada drought, Vidarbha drought

### 3. **Hydrological Drought**
- **Characteristics**: Water shortage, depleted reservoirs, critical water crisis
- **Resource Impact**: Extreme water need, significant food aid, medical for waterborne diseases
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.2×, Shelter 0.5×, Medical 0.9×, Water 1.8×
- **Examples**: Severe water crises in Tamil Nadu, Karnataka

### 4. **Socioeconomic Drought**
- **Characteristics**: Economic impact, food insecurity, migration
- **Resource Impact**: High food aid, moderate shelter (for migrants), water critical
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.4×, Shelter 0.6×, Medical 1.0×, Water 1.7×
- **Examples**: Long-term drought impacts in Bundelkhand

## 🌊 Tsunami Sub-Types

### 1. **Tectonic Tsunami**
- **Characteristics**: Earthquake-triggered, widespread, high wave energy
- **Resource Impact**: Large area affected, critical medical and shelter need
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.2×, Shelter 1.3×, Medical 1.4×, Water 1.1×
- **Examples**: 2004 Indian Ocean Tsunami

### 2. **Volcanic Tsunami**
- **Characteristics**: Volcanic activity triggered, ash fall, gas hazards
- **Resource Impact**: Critical medical need (respiratory), high shelter demand
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.3×, Shelter 1.4×, Medical 1.5×, Water 1.2×
- **Examples**: Krakatoa-type events (rare in India)

### 3. **Landslide-Triggered Tsunami**
- **Characteristics**: Localized but intense, high wave energy in specific area
- **Resource Impact**: Intense medical response, significant shelter need
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.1×, Shelter 1.2×, Medical 1.3×, Water 1.0×
- **Examples**: Landslide-generated waves in Andaman & Nicobar

### 4. **Meteorite Tsunami**
- **Characteristics**: Rare, impact-generated, standard response protocols
- **Resource Impact**: Standard resource allocation
- **Modifiers**: Food 1.0×, Shelter 1.0×, Medical 1.0×, Water 1.0×
- **Examples**: Extremely rare events

## 🎯 Selection Guidelines

### When to Select Each Sub-Type

1. **Check Disaster Characteristics**:
   - Speed of onset (flash vs gradual)
   - Area affected (localized vs widespread)
   - Intensity (weak vs extreme)
   - Depth/height (shallow vs deep)

2. **Consider Geographic Context**:
   - Urban vs rural
   - Coastal vs inland
   - Mountain vs plain
   - Forest vs grassland

3. **Review Historical Similar Events**:
   - What sub-type was it?
   - What resources were actually needed?
   - How did it differ from standard predictions?

4. **When Unsure**:
   - Leave blank (system uses default)
   - Default assumes standard/typical characteristics
   - Better to be conservative than overestimate
